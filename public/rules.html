How to play

Rules

Build the quickest route from the departure station to the arrival station. Each day has five puzzles, worth up to 100 points each.

Build your route

  1. Choose a line from the departure station.
  2. Pick a direction and the stop where you want to get off.
  3. Change lines or use a walking connection when one is available.
  4. Keep going until you reach the arrival station.

You can reset an unfinished route. If you give up, the game shows the fastest route and gives you 0 points for that puzzle.

How time works

Your total includes time on the train, estimated waits, and the time needed to change stations or lines. These numbers come from the game's fixed timetable data and timing model. They are not live travel estimates.

Scoring

You get 100 points when your route exactly matches the stored fastest route. Otherwise, the score drops based on the extra time in minutes and as a percentage of the fastest time.

Score = 100 − 3 × minutes slower − 0.6 × percentage slower

The 3 and 0.6 are just balancing choices. If you reach the destination, you score between 10 and 99 unless your route is an exact match. Giving up scores 0. After five puzzles, the game adds everything together for a score out of 500.
