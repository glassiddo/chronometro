About Chronométro

Chronométro is a daily puzzle where you find the fastest route between two stations. Choose which lines to take and where to change, then see how your journey compares with the fastest route in the game. Each city has five puzzles a day.

Data

Paris: routes come from an ITO World GTFS export based on Île-de-France Mobilités data. The snapshot is version 20260630_200738 and covers 27 June to 29 July 2026.

London: Underground, Elizabeth line, and DLR route data comes from a saved Transport for London Unified API snapshot. Underground and DLR runtimes use timetable intervals; Elizabeth line runtimes use saved Journey Planner results where timetable data was unavailable.

Chicago: data is from the Chicago Transit Authority static GTFS schedule downloaded 6 August 2026, valid 5 August through 31 October 2026. Data provided by Chicago Transit Authority. Use is subject to the CTA developer terms and GTFS information. Chronométro does not use live Train Tracker data.

Washington: data is from WMATA's official static rail GTFS snapshot downloaded 28 August 2026. feed_info.txt covers scheduled service from 26 August through 4 September 2026; the calendar records extend through 2 January 2027. Use is subject to the WMATA Transit Data Terms of Use. No live or real-time WMATA data is used.

The game does not use live data and may be wrong about current routes, times, closures, stopping patterns, or accessibility.

Privacy

There are no accounts or cookies, and the game does not save your progress in the browser. Routes and scores disappear when you reload or close the page.
