About Chronométro

Data

The routes come from an ITO World GTFS export based on Île-de-France Mobilités data. The snapshot is version 20260630_200738 and covers 27 June to 29 July 2026.

The game does not use live data and may be wrong about current routes, times, closures, or accessibility.

Privacy

There are no accounts or cookies, and the game does not save your progress in the browser. Routes and scores disappear when you reload or close the page.

Hosted by Netlify.

Contact

Found a bug or a problem with a route? Open an issue on GitHub.
